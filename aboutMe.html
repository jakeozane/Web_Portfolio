About Me

I'm a Senior Support Engineer based in the Greater Chicago Area with 5+ years of experience keeping customer-facing SaaS platforms reliable. At Pricefx, I work alongside engineering teams to track down critical bugs in an enterprise pricing platform: debugging client-configured Groovy logic, digging into PostgreSQL and MariaDB performance issues, and monitoring cloud and bare-metal Linux production environments with Grafana and Kibana.

I enjoy the part of the job where the problem isn't obvious yet: reading logs, forming a hypothesis, and following it down to a root cause. I also care about making the team around me better. I lead onboarding and mentorship for new Support Engineers, keep our Confluence documentation current, and step in as acting lead for the US Support Team when needed.

My path into engineering started with a B.S. in Psychology and a minor in Informatics at the University of Illinois, followed by Computer Science coursework at Oregon State University. That mix still shapes how I work: I try to understand both the system and the person on the other end of the ticket.

Outside of work, I'm into computer hardware. I built my own PC and spend time tuning my Ender 3 3D printer.
